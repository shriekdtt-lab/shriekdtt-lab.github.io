Privacy Policy — Last updated: August 25, 2026

Shriekd operates automated content accounts on YouTube and TikTok. This privacy policy explains how we handle data in connection with our content and our use of third-party platform APIs.

Data We Collect

Shriekd does not collect, store, or sell any personal data from viewers or followers. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics beyond what the platforms (YouTube, TikTok) provide natively to all creators.

TikTok API Usage

Shriekd uses the TikTok Content Posting API solely to upload short-form video clips to our own @shriekd TikTok account on an automated schedule. We do not access, store, or process any user data, follower data, or third-party account information. The app acts solely on behalf of the Shriekd account owner.

Third Party Platforms

Content is sourced from Twitch, YouTube, and Reddit using their public APIs. We do not store personal information about the creators whose clips we share beyond what is publicly available on those platforms. All clips include creator credit and a link to the original source.

Children's Privacy

Shriekd content is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or store data from minors.

Data Retention

No personal data is retained by Shriekd. API tokens used for automated posting are stored locally on a private device and are not shared with any third parties.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us through our TikTok or YouTube channel.
